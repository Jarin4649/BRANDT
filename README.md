**BRANDT v. 0.0.1**

Smart Car Technology

Understanding Your Car Beyond the Display Screen

---

**Overview**

Brandt is a smart car technology platform designed to make information about cars, automotive engineering, and technical knowledge more accessible and understandable.

By integrating car data, intelligent assistance systems, and automotive knowledge, Brandt aims to bridge the gap between complex car systems and the average user.

**Problem**

Modern cars are becoming increasingly complex. Understanding car systems, diagnostic information, and automotive engineering can be difficult for users without a technical background.

Key car information isn't always easily interpreted or applied to everyday situations. Some applications don't explain each component in a way that users with limited car knowledge can understand.

**Our Approach**

Brandt is committed to transforming complex automotive data into clear, easy-to-understand, and useful insights.

At the heart of this experience is MACK, an intelligent assistant, a personal car advisor designed to make automotive knowledge more accessible.

**Key Features**

*MACK Intelligent Assistant*

An AI-powered automotive assistant designed to explain car-related concepts and help users understand technical information. Users can ask questions about their concerns or observations in the vehicle without using excessive automotive jargon. MACK will diagnose and explain, or answer questions using simple terminology.

*Automotive Diagnostics*

A automotive diagnostic experience designed with automotive data and OBD-II integration in mind.

*Live Data*

A dedicated interface for displaying automotive system data when compatible diagnostic data is available.

*Intelligent Visualization System*

Visual descriptions designed to easily understand complex automotive engineering concepts.

**Technologies Used**

- Kotlin
- Android application development
- AI-powered help desk
- OBD-II Bluetooth/Wi-Fi integration
- Firebase-related project components

**Development Status**

BRANDT is under ongoing development.

Some features are still in rollout, integration, and testing. Hardware compatibility, real-time automotive connectivity, and diagnostic functions require further verification.

**Future Features**

- A system allowing users to find nearby garages by specifying their GPS coordinates, etc., and recommending garages.
- Ability to customize vehicle performance, such as Remax, etc.

**Vision**

Our vision is to make automotive technology easier to understand by connecting engineering knowledge and automotive data. And intelligent assistance systems are integrated into a single platform.
